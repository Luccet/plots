On the 80 days of 16 °C or more it only heats water, 2.4 kWh a day, and the line comes down to that at about 15.3 °C, where the heating starts. If the heat pump's seasonal efficiency is 3, those 1.3 kWh of electricity are about 3.9 kWh of heat a day for each degree: a heat loss of about 160 W per degree between inside and out.
